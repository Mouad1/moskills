# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The maintainer first, then community builders who juggle several projects with AI coding agents.
They open the moskills dashboard on a laptop next to their editor (sometimes on a phone) to see,
at a glance, what agents did, what waits for their review, and to start the next task.
Sessions are short and frequent: check, decide, move on.

## Product Purpose

moskills keeps agent work deliberate: clear intent, verified evidence, resumable progress, clean
handoffs. The dashboard is a local, zero-dependency window onto that record, extended by plug-in
panels from other tools (e.g. MoOS). Success: the user trusts every status shown because it comes
from a file, and can act (review, accept, start) without hunting through folders.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, trustworthy. Quiet surfaces, plain language, clear status. It never shouts; it
tells you exactly what is true and what is still not done.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS analytics: big hero numbers, gradient charts, identical KPI card grids.
- Cream "AI editorial": beige/parchment backgrounds with serif headlines everywhere.

## Design Principles

1. Evidence over decoration: every number or status traces to a saved file; no vanity metrics.
2. What needs me first: review-waiting and failed items lead; "all quiet" is a valid, calm state.
3. One place, many panels: panels feel native to one system; a broken panel never breaks the page.
4. Local and safe by default: nothing leaves the machine; actions are explicit and confirmed.
5. Plain words: labels say what happens ("Accept result", "Start thread"), not jargon.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: text contrast ≥ 4.5:1 (≥ 3:1 for large text and UI boundaries), full keyboard use
with visible focus, status never conveyed by color alone, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected,
usable at 375 px width without horizontal scroll.
